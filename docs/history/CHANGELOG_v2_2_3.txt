ESP32 Wireless Dongle v2.2.3
==============================

A2DP media-path regression fix and diagnostics.

Changes:
- Restored the proven v1.9-style A2DP test-tone callback path.
- Test tone is generated whenever tone mode is enabled, independently of PC audio buffering.
- A2DPSource volume is explicitly set to 127 when the source starts.
- A2DP status now reports the library-reported connection/audio state.
- Added internal A2DP audio callback counters for firmware diagnostics.
- Preserves Wi-Fi, TCP, UDP, Classic SPP, Classic GAP scan, BLE scan, cached A2DP reconnect, and PC-audio architecture.
