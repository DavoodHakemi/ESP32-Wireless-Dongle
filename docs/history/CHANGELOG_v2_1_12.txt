ESP32 Wireless Dongle v2.1.12

- Reverted PC audio transport to one ADPCM block per UART frame for smoother pacing.
- Increased ESP32 audio ring buffer to 32 KiB.
- Kept 30-block prebuffer.
- Preserved all existing Wi-Fi, TCP/UDP, Bluetooth Classic/BLE/SPP, A2DP, tone and protocol features.
- Kept UART fixed at 921600 baud and IMA ADPCM transport.
